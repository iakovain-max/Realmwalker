Sounds in Realmwalker
=====================

Every sound here is in the public domain (Creative Commons CC0 1.0,
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Thank you to their makers:

  RPG Audio by Kenney (kenney.nl)  -  https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio
  Interface Sounds by Kenney (kenney.nl)  -  https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
  Impact Sounds by Kenney (kenney.nl)  -  https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
  RPG Sound Pack by artisticdude  -  https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-sound-pack
  80 CC0 RPG SFX by rubberduck  -  https://opengameart.org/content/80-cc0-rpg-sfx
  100 CC0 SFX by rubberduck  -  https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx

The bow, the level-up harp, the journal's notes, the chimes, the drum and the magic thud were made
for Realmwalker by tools/build_sounds.py (also CC0).

Each sound was trimmed, softened and brought to the same loudness by tools/build_sounds.py.
