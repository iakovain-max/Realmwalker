Music in Realmwalker
====================

The tunes that come with the game are by RandomMind (opengameart.org/users/randommind),
in the public domain (Creative Commons CC0 1.0). Thank you!

  title/exploration.ogg          Medieval: Exploration  -  https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-exploration
  travel/harvest_season.ogg      Medieval: Harvest Season  -  https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-harvest-season
  town/market_day.ogg            Medieval: Market Day  -  https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-market-day
  town/rejoicing.ogg             Medieval: Rejoicing  -  https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-rejoicing
  tavern/minstrel_dance.ogg      Medieval: Minstrel Dance  -  https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-minstrel-dance
  combat/battle.ogg              Medieval: Battle  -  https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-battle
  victory/victory.ogg            Medieval: Victory Theme  -  https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-victory-theme
  defeat/defeat.ogg              Medieval: Defeat Theme  -  https://opengameart.org/content/medieval-defeat-theme

Each was brought to the same loudness (-18 LUFS) by tools/build_sounds.py.
Your own music goes in the 'music' folder next to the game, not here (see the README there).
